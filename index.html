Spout calibration

Put the spout on a bottle or container of water and grab something with a measured line: a jigger for liquor spouts, a mixing tin or measuring cup for juice and mixer containers. Hold the pour button the whole time you pour exactly to the line, then let go. Do it 3 times and the app averages that spout's real speed.
